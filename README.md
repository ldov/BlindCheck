# BlindCheck — Blind AI Evaluator Game

A casual blind-evaluation game where everyday players test, inspect, and spot flaws in responses generated by Artificial Intelligence models.

## What is the Game?

**BlindCheck** turns AI quality assurance into a fast, accessible, and gamified experience.

Instead of traditional side-by-side comparisons (Model A vs. Model B), the player receives a **single response** generated by a mystery AI for a randomly selected prompt or task. The player acts as a quality inspector: reading the interaction and deciding whether the response passes the test or contains a flaw (such as fabricated information, going off-topic, unnecessary fluff, or an unnatural robotic tone).

With every completed mission, players earn points, level up, maintain streaks, and reveal which AI model was behind the response.

## How the Program Works

The system operates in a simple, continuous four-step loop:

1. **Random Draw (Blind Testing):** When a mission starts, the system randomly picks a prompt/task from a challenge pool and selects an available AI model to generate the response. The model's identity remains hidden throughout the evaluation to eliminate brand bias.

2. **Player Inspection:** The prompt and response are displayed on screen. The player evaluates the usefulness and quality of the content, marking whether the response is accurate or flagging specific errors using quick, intuitive categories (with an optional short comment).

3. **Gamification & Reveal:** After submitting their verdict, the player earns points for the mission (with bonuses for detailed feedback and consistency), tracks their overall progress, and discovers which AI model they just evaluated.

4. **Data Structuring:** Every completed round generates a comprehensive record containing the evaluator's ID, the selected task, the model used, the generated response, and the detailed feedback—stored in a structured format (JSON or database) ready for analysis.

## Project Goal

The primary goal of **BlindCheck** is to capture **authentic, granular, and unbiased human feedback** on how AI models perform in everyday scenarios.

While traditional benchmarks only show which model won a head-to-head matchup, this project focuses on **diagnosing the exact reason behind failures** from the perspective of regular users rather than just technical specialists. The collected data serves as a strategic resource for AI companies and researchers looking to:

* Identify hallucination patterns, reasoning flaws, and lack of natural language fluency;
* Evaluate models across unpredictable and diverse real-world scenarios;
* Feed continuous improvement pipelines, fine-tuning, and human preference alignment (RLHF / DPO);
* Scale the exact same inspection mechanic in the future to multimodal evaluations (audio and image).
